Our Story

Cooling Homes Since 2014

CoolBreeze AC Services was founded with a simple mission: to provide fast, honest, and reliable air conditioning solutions to homes and businesses across the city.

What started as a small team of two technicians has grown into a full-service company with a dedicated team of certified engineers who handle everything from routine maintenance to complex installations.

We take pride in our transparent approach — we explain every job before we start and never hit you with unexpected charges.
