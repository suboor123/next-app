AI Agent Development

AI agents are intelligent systems capable of performing tasks autonomously by combining reasoning, memory, and external tool usage. I design and build AI agents that interact with APIs, automate workflows, and execute complex tasks using modern LLM architectures.

What Are AI Agents

AI agents extend large language models by enabling them to take actions, use tools and make decisions. These systems combine LLM reasoning with external integrations such as APIs, databases, automation tools and knowledge systems.

  • AI-driven automation systems
