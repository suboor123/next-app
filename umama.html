My Dearest Wife

✦

My love, my heart, my everything —

I sit here searching for the perfect words, but I know that no words could ever fully capture the depth of what I feel for you. You are the most precious person in my world — more than you may realise, more than I sometimes show.

These past months have been hard. Life pressed in from every side — stress, exhaustion, worry — and somewhere in all of that, I let it spill over onto you. I was short with you. I raised my voice when you deserved patience. I pulled away when you needed warmth.

And I am so deeply, genuinely sorry for that. You never deserved any of it. Not a single sharp word, not a single cold silence. You deserved tenderness every single day — and I failed you in those moments.

But I need you to know something, with every fibre of my being: I love you more than anyone on this earth. You are not just my wife — you are my safe place, my favourite person, the one my soul chose. That has never changed. Not for a single second.

You are so strong, so beautiful, so full of grace. Watching you move through life fills me with a quiet awe that I don't say nearly enough. You make ordinary days feel like something worth treasuring.

Please keep holding my hand — I am still learning to be the partner you deserve. ♥
