The Pipeline

01. INGESTION & CHUNKING

Data Decomposition

Large scale documents are parsed, cleaned, and broken into optimized overlapping segments to preserve semantic context.

02. EMBEDDINGS

Vectorization

Transforming text into high-dimensional numerical arrays using state-of-the-art embedding models.

03. INDEXING

Vector Search

Fast HNSW or IVF indexing in databases like Pinecone, Milvus, or Weaviate for sub-second retrieval.

04. AUGMENTATION

Knowledge-Driven Generation

The LLM receives the prompt + the retrieved context, allowing it to cite sources and provide factual, ground-truth answers.
